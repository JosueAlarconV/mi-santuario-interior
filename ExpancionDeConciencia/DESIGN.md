## Brand & Style

This design system crafts a sanctuary for the contemporary esoteric experience—a digital sacred space where cosmic exploration meets serene ritual. It balances transcendental mysticism with high-end luxury, targeting seekers, astrological practitioners, sound healers, and digital occultists. The UI inspires quiet awe, introspective intimacy, and celestial wonder.

The aesthetic fuses **Glassmorphism** and **Atmospheric Surrealism**:
- Deep cosmic fields evoke the boundless expanse of the void.
- Translucent veil layers mirror celestial phenomena and subtle energy fields.
- Luminous glows and ethereal edge-lighting introduce an astral, electric pulse without degrading visual focus or clarity.
- Restraint governs all interactions: elements breathe with celestial pacing, eschewing harsh digital snappiness in favor of fluid, meditative presence.

## Colors

The palette adheres strictly to a 60-30-10 celestial hierarchy calibrated for deep nocturnal contemplation:

- **Dominant Core (60%):** `#0F051D` (Deep Astral Void) and `#1A0A2A` (Cosmic Obsidian). These shades compose the foundation, viewport backdrop, master shells, and underlying atmospheric canvases. They simulate the vast darkness of space, stabilizing the eye and framing illuminated content.
- **Secondary Structure (30%):** `#9D4EDD` (Electric Amethyst) alongside `#8A2BE2` (Nebula Violet). Deployed across glass surface borders, interactive states, secondary glyphs, structural dividers, and softly radiant gradients. They supply mystical presence without competing with foreground text.
- **Accent Illumination (10%):** `#FF77A8` (Esoteric Magenta) backed by `#E0AAFF` (Luminous Lavender Aura). Reserved strictly for focal interactive CTAs, active spiritual indicators, astral badges, and intense focal glows.
- **Neutral & Text Layers:** `#F7F2FA` (Ethereal Starlight) provides razor-sharp legibility across all typography tiers on dark surfaces. A muted starlight variant (`rgba(247, 242, 250, 0.65)`) delivers gentle hierarchy for secondary narratives and metaphysical metadata.

## Typography

Typography bridges timeless esoteric literature and modern interface utility. 

- **Display & Headline:** `Playfair Display` channels high-end editorial sophistication, Grimoire incipits, and fine astrological folios. Large headers lean into italicized accents on featured words to evoke cosmic cadence.
- **Body & Labels:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides geometric clarity and rounded softness that harmonizes with cosmic geometry without competing with the serifs.
- **Micro-Copy & Sacred Badges:** Render labels in uppercase with extended letter spacing (`0.08em` to `0.15em`) to evoke carved planetary talismans and classical inscriptions.

## Layout & Spacing

A spiritual digital environment requires substantial whitespace—or "astral breathing room"—to sustain meditation and clarity:

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop layouts employ a 12-column fluid grid, scaling down to an 8-column layout on tablet and a 4-column layout on mobile.
- **Margins & Pacing:** Outlying margins remain spacious (`3rem` on desktop), establishing an uninterrupted celestial void surrounding foreground modules.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Section gaps span generous multiples of `space-xl` (ranging from 4rem to 7rem on desktop viewports) to prevent cognitive noise and let rituals unfold one at a time.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth mimics looking through celestial spheres rather than stacking plastic sheets. The hierarchy relies on glassmorphism and radiant cosmic energy:

- **Void Layer (0dp):** Solid deep background `#0F051D` layered with subtle, slow-drifting cosmic radial gradients (`#1A0A2A` shifting into deep indigo).
- **Glass Veils (1dp - 2dp):** Card containers and panels utilize `rgba(26, 10, 42, 0.55)` paired with a `16px` to `24px` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`). 
- **Ethereal Borders:** Outlines mimic delicate crystalline filaments using a 1px border gradient transitioning from `rgba(157, 78, 221, 0.45)` at the top-left to `rgba(255, 119, 168, 0.12)` at the bottom-right.
- **Astral Glow & Luminescence (3dp+):** Interactive focal points, active states, and floating sheets shed diffuse light rather than grey cast shadows. They employ soft atmospheric auras: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(157, 78, 221, 0.35), 0 0 16px 2px rgba(255, 119, 168, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a refined **Rounded** contour (`0.5rem` / `8px` base, scaling to `1rem` / `16px` for cards and modals, and full pill-shapes for tactile badges).

Curves soften digital geometry into organic celestial forms—echoing lunar arcs, planetary orbits, and polished crystals. Avoid stark, aggressive right angles; every container represents a smooth glass portal or polished talisman.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Talismans
- **Primary Celestial Action:** Filled with a gradient from `#FF77A8` to `#9D4EDD`. Text is set in `#F7F2FA` with `label-lg` styling. Outer glow manifests as `0 4px 20px rgba(255, 119, 168, 0.4)`. On hover, scale expands by 1.02 with an intenser inner lavender radiance.
- **Secondary Astral Action:** Frosted veil background (`rgba(157, 78, 221, 0.15)`), framed by a 1px border in `#9D4EDD`. On hover, border illuminates to `#E0AAFF` with an internal soft aura.
- **Ghost Action:** Pure typography in `#E0AAFF` flanked by delicate astral glyphs (stars, crescent phases) with an animated bottom highlight glow.

### Cards & Altar Modules
- Built on translucent cosmic obsidian (`rgba(26, 10, 42, 0.6)`), rounded to `1rem` (`rounded-lg`), backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`.
- Features an ultra-fine 1px perimeter border tinted with `#9D4EDD` at 30% opacity.
- Hover states initiate a subtle vertical lift accompanied by a diffuse `#8A2BE2` halo behind the card.

### Chips & Constellation Tags
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) modules with `space-xs` vertical and `space-md` horizontal padding.
- Neutral state: `rgba(247, 242, 250, 0.06)` background, `1px solid rgba(224, 170, 255, 0.25)`, text in `#E0AAFF`.
- Active state: `#9D4EDD` surface with an esoteric magenta accent dot (`#FF77A8`) and sharp `#F7F2FA` text.

### Form Inputs & Query Fields
- Input surfaces adopt dark translucent wells: `rgba(15, 5, 29, 0.75)` with a 1px border in `rgba(157, 78, 221, 0.3)`.
- Placeholder text glows softly in `rgba(247, 242, 250, 0.35)`.
- Active focus state transitions the border to `#FF77A8` and emits an exterior neon violet focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(157, 78, 221, 0.25)`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Distinctive circular astrological geometry for both controls.
- Checkbox: When checked, transitions from a dim astral ring to a vibrant `#9D4EDD` core accented with a centered `#FF77A8` starlight spark.
- Radio: Outer ring shines in `#8A2BE2`; active inner orb glows in `#FF77A8` with a miniature diffuse halo.

### Specialized Sacred Components
- **Zodiac & Phase Carousel:** Horizontally floating frosted glass nodes displaying celestial positions, illuminated via variable intensity violet light gradients matching the current lunar phase.
- **Altar Modal & Portal:** Full-screen overlay in `rgba(15, 5, 29, 0.85)` with heavy blur (`32px`), presenting centered mystical tools, tarot spreads, or cosmic readings inside glowing gradient envelopes.
